Start with: python local-app/server.py --open (from the repository root).
On Windows, you can also double-click Open Study Studio.cmd.

The app is at http://127.0.0.1:8768 and saves to study-progress.sqlite3
in this directory. Keep this file to preserve your checks, notes and hours.
It is intentionally excluded from Git. A fresh clone starts with no progress.

Read ../README.md for complete setup, backup, development and hosting details.
